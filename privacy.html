Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

Thought Of is being developed as a private relationship-maintenance application. This policy describes the information the service is designed to use and the principles that govern that use during private beta and future release.

Information we use

Thought Of may process information you provide, such as your phone number, display name, profile photo, Thoughts, reactions, private group names, connection preferences, and settings. The service may also process technical information needed to operate securely, such as authentication records, device or push-notification tokens, timestamps, and limited diagnostic logs.

Contacts

Thought Of does not import or upload your address book. To request a connection, a user manually enters a phone number. The service is designed not to reveal whether an entered number already belongs to a Thought Of account.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide mutual connections, deliver Thoughts and reactions, maintain private groups and archives, prevent abuse, and operate and secure the service.

SMS verification

Automated SMS from Thought Of is used for phone-number authentication. A verification text is sent only after a person enters their own phone number and requests a code. Thought Of does not automatically send third-party invitation texts. Invitations may instead be composed in the sender's own messaging application, where the sender chooses whether to send them.

Sharing and sale of data

Thought Of is not designed to sell personal information or make private Thoughts public. Service providers may process information only as needed to provide infrastructure such as authentication, database hosting, security, messaging delivery, file storage, and push notifications.

Retention and control

Connection history is designed to be controlled independently by each user. Archived connection history may be retained for up to one year unless deleted sooner. Additional retention may be necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and operational backups.

Security

Thought Of uses access controls and server-side authorization designed to limit users to data they are entitled to access. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the service is designed to minimize unnecessary collection and exposure.

Children

Thought Of is not intended for children under 13.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the private beta develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page before broader public release.
