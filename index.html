thought of

For the people you think about between conversations.

Thought Of is a private, lightweight way to stay close between real conversations through small, low-pressure messages called Thoughts. There is no public feed, no follower count, and no contact importing.

Thought Of is operated by Terrance Mann, sole proprietor.

Your People

Connections are mutual and private. People are added by entering a phone number manually. Thought Of never imports your address book.

Small Thoughts

Share a brief Thought when someone crosses your mind without turning it into a full conversation.

Reach Out

When it is time for a real conversation, Thought Of hands you back to the messaging apps you already use.
