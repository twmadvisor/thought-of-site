thought of

For the people you think about between conversations.

Thought Of is a private, lightweight way to keep a connection warm without turning every thought into a full conversation. No public feed. No follower counts. No contact importing.

Currently in private development

Your People

Connections are mutual and private. People are added by entering a phone number manually. Thought Of never imports your address book.

Small Thoughts

Share a brief Thought between real conversations. The product is intentionally designed to be low-pressure rather than attention-maximizing.

Reach Out

When it is time for a real conversation, Thought Of hands you back to the messaging apps you already use.
